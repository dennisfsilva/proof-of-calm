Proof of Calm notices a fall from prices, never from headlines. It counts one when the coins you
hold, in the amounts you hold, fall 10 % or more in seven days, or close 30 % or more below their
highest close of the last 90 days. Today then shows one line on one visit, and Learn opens with the
fall under way.

## How often

On bitcoin, from 16 Oct 2021 to 16 Oct 2026, a seven-day fall of 10 % or more came on 5.7 % of
days, about 6 episodes a year; a close 30 % or more below the 90-day high came on 11.8 % of days;
either one came on 15.1 % of days.

Proof of Calm's own computation on Bitstamp BTC/USD daily closes. <PastPerformance />

## Monthly plans through falls

Proof of Calm also computed monthly plans on Bitstamp BTC/USD daily closes: $100 on the 1st of each
month, no fee, valued 1 month after the last buy. Every four-year plan in this data, 118 start
months from Jan 2013 to Oct 2022, ended above cost; the worst at 1.37×, for buys from Jan 2019 to
Dec 2022. Along the way, 26 % of them spent 12 or more month-ends in a row below cost.

Another simulation points the other way. BIS Working Paper 1049 (14 Nov 2022) took people who
downloaded a crypto app from August 2015 to June 2022, valued at October 2022 prices: buying $100
of bitcoin a month from download, 81 % would have lost money, and the median investor would have
lost 48 % of $900 invested. Where a window starts and ends decides much of the result.

## What a monthly buyer can do

A monthly buyer can do nothing, log the buy on the buy day, or re-read the plan. Proof of Calm
suggests no purchase and no sale, and places no orders.

## The fall under way

While a fall of the coins you hold is under way, Learn's first row opens a page with the figure,
each coin's earlier falls, and the next buy day. When none is, that page's address opens this
module.
