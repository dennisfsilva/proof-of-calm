Proof of Calm shows no news feed, no sentiment index, and no price notifications. Each is built to
bring a person back to the screen. A monthly plan exists so that no headline decides a purchase.

## What a sentiment index is

A sentiment index turns the market's mood into one number. alternative.me's Crypto Fear & Greed
Index covers bitcoin only, from 0 to 100, with daily data from 1 Feb 2018. It mixes 6 inputs, as
published: volatility 25 %, momentum and volume 25 %, social media 15 %, surveys 15 %, dominance
10 %, and Google Trends 10 %. Surveys are marked "currently paused", and alternative.me does not
say where their 15 % goes.

CoinMarketCap launched its own index on 3 Aug 2023, with 5 components and weights it does not
disclose. On 16 Oct 2026 alternative.me's index read 63, "Greed", and CoinMarketCap's 50, "Neutral":
13 points apart on the same day.

## Why traders watch them

A trader acts on moves of hours or days, so the market's mood is part of the trade. A monthly buyer
has set the amount and the day in advance, and a reading of 63 or 50 changes neither. Two indexes
13 points apart on one day also show how much the number depends on who builds it.

## A headline and the plan

A monthly plan needs nothing from a headline. The next purchase falls on the buy day, at the amount
the plan sets, whatever the headline said.

## What the app shows instead

Proof of Calm shows one line on Today, once per fall, worked out from prices, never from
headlines. It counts a fall when the coins you hold lose 10 % or more in seven days or close
30 % or more below their 90‑day high. The line names the fall and links to a page with the figure and each coin's earlier
falls. It shows on one visit per fall, on this device.
