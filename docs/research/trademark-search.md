# Trade-mark search for "Proof of Calm", 2026-11-09

`docs/LEGAL.md` §1 asks for a TMview search for "Proof of Calm" and close variants in classes 9, 36,
41 and 42 before anything public ships (ADR-0040). The owner delegated the item on 2026-11-09, and an
agent ran the search that day. This is a record of what the registers hold, not a legal opinion.

## Sources

- TMview's public search endpoint, about 110 searches: the EUIPO, every EU national office, WIPO's
  Madrid records, the US, the UK and other offices. The web page needs JavaScript, and the site began
  refusing this machine's requests after the searches.
- EUIPO eSearch plus: EU trade marks and international registrations designating the EU, with their
  goods and services.
- Not read: WIPO's Global Brand Database (a proof-of-work captcha) and INPI France's record pages
  (HTTP 403).
- A web search for "Proof of Calm" and "proofofcalm" found no use of the name.

## What this search cannot show

It cannot show:
- unregistered use, or company and domain names;
- applications not yet published, or a lag in TMview's feeds;
- figurative marks whose words were not indexed, and offices outside TMview.

Nor can it say whether an office or a court would find any of these marks "confusingly similar"
(`docs/LEGAL.md` §1). That is a legal judgment, and the professional clearance opinion that §1
calls optional is where it is made. The filing itself stays the owner's yes/no (money and their
legal identity).
