# Filing the trade mark "Proof of Calm", 2026-11-19

`docs/LEGAL.md` §1 asks for an EU trade mark once the search is done
(`docs/research/trademark-search.md`). What does a filing cost, which classes fit the product, what
must the application contain, and is there a cheaper route? An agent read the texts on 2026-11-19,
on the owner's delegation of 2026-11-18. This is a record of fees and rules, not a legal opinion.

## EUIPO's fees

An individual EU trade mark filed online costs EUR 850 with one class, EUR 50 for the second class
and EUR 150 for each class beyond two (Annex I, A.1 to A.4): EUR 900 for two classes, EUR 1,050 for
three, EUR 1,200 for four. A word mark and a figurative mark cost the same, and each is its own
application. The fee is due within one month of filing for the filing date to hold.

## The classes

- **Class 9**, the installable app: "computer software applications, downloadable" (090717),
  "computer programs, downloadable" (090658), "electronic publications, downloadable" (090657).
- **Class 41**, the Learn modules and country pages: "teaching / educational services / instruction
  services" (410017), "providing information in the field of education" (410048), "providing online
  electronic publications, not downloadable" (410099), "online publication of electronic books and
  journals" (410091).
- **Class 42**, the web app: "software as a service [SaaS]" (420220), "providing online
  non-downloadable computer software" (420300).
- **Class 36, weighed.** Its heading is "Financial, monetary and banking services; insurance
  services; real estate services." It holds "providing financial information" (360059) and
  "providing financial information via a website" (360113), which describe information without
  advice, beside "financial advice" (360137), "financial management" (360030) and "financial trading
  of cryptocurrency" (360140), which describe services the product does not offer. The search of
  2026-11-09 found in this class the registered word mark CALM (EUTM 014834733) and a pending
  figurative PROOF (EUTM application 019370556, classes 9 and 36). As a third or fourth class it
  costs EUR 150, at filing only: the applicant may restrict a filed list at any time, and an
  amendment may not extend it (Regulation (EU) 2017/1001 art. 49(1) and (2), read on 2026-11-23 in
  the text as adopted, in the UK's copy at
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/1001/article/49/adopted, since EUR-Lex answered with an
  empty page that day). After filing, class 36 is a new application at EUR 850.

## The application and the register

The application holds the applicant's name (a natural person's family and given names), address and
State of domicile, the list of goods and services, a representation of the mark, a first language
and a second one among English, French, German, Italian and Spanish, and a signature (Implementing
Regulation (EU) 2018/626 art. 2(1)). An applicant domiciled in the EEA needs no representative. The
applicant's name and address are entered in the register, which is open to public inspection
(Regulation (EU) 2017/1001 art. 111).

## After filing

EUIPO examines the formalities and the absolute grounds, then publishes the application. An earlier
mark's owner may oppose within three months of publication, for a fee of EUR 320. With no opposition
the mark is registered for 10 years from the filing date. An opposition opens with a cooling-off
period of two months, which both parties can extend to 24 months in total: an applicant who
withdraws or restricts the list in that period bears no costs. An applicant who loses after it bears
the opponent's fee and representation costs capped at EUR 300, so at most EUR 620 beside its own
costs (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/625 art. 6; Regulation (EU) 2017/1001 art. 109).

## The SME Fund 2026

- Its trade-mark voucher reimburses 75 % of trade-mark fees at national and EU level, up to EUR 700
  per beneficiary, one voucher per call (GR/001/26, 1.3 and 1.4).
- The applicant is an SME established in the EU. A self-employed person qualifies with an official
  certificate of economic activity; the call has no provision for a private person without one (1.6,
  3.2).
- The grant comes first: fees paid before the grant is notified are not reimbursed (1.7). The mark
  is then filed and paid within one month of the notification, two with the one extension (4.1).
- The voucher ran out earlier in 2026, reopened on 2026-11-05 and was announced to close on
  2026-11-19 at 17:00 CET. No 2027 fund was announced.

## The national route

INPI Portugal charges EUR 151.98 for an online application with one class and EUR 38.52 for each
further class, so EUR 229.02 for three classes; a registration fee is paid on grant. A national
filing gives six months in which a later EU application can claim its date (Regulation (EU)
2017/1001 art. 34(1)).

Three totals for classes 9, 41 and 42:

| Route | Cost |
|---|---|
| The EU filing directly | EUR 1,050 |
| INPI first, the EU filing within six months, no grant | EUR 1,279.02 plus INPI's registration fee |
| The same two steps with a grant of EUR 700 | EUR 579.02 plus INPI's registration fee |
